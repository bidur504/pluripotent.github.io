About the Foundation

The Pluripotent Foundation exists so that every student has access to quality mentorship, no matter where they live. Our team of volunteer professionals bridges the gap between students in underserved communities and people who have walked the paths those students dream of.

Our Vision

Every student should have access to quality mentorship, no matter where they live.

Our Mission

To connect students with mentors online, guiding them toward the right career path and a meaningful life.

Our Values

  • Motivation Encouraging students to do their best.
  • Integrity Being honest and transparent in what we do.
  • Innovation Using new and creative ways to mentor.

What we do

Our Work

We ensure that students from remote or underserved areas receive guidance from experienced mentors, regardless of their location. Through online platforms like Zoom, meaningful learning, career guidance, and mentorship can happen anytime, anywhere.

  1. Personal Mentorship

    Regular virtual, one-to-one and group interactions that help students identify and grow their talents.

  2. Engaging Learning

    Debates, quizzes, and mock tests that make learning active, measurable, and fun.

  3. Life Skills

    A strong focus on communication, discipline, and confidence — beyond academics.

  4. Digital Learning

    Zoom classes in Basic IT, Basic Mathematics, Loksewa (Public Service) preparation, and English speaking practice using ChatGPT.

  5. Mentor Training

    Training sessions that keep the quality of guidance high, attended by 15 mentors between January and August 2025.

Looking ahead

Two new initiatives

We are launching the Talent Search Examination and Super 30 to identify talented students and provide them with focused mentorship, academic support, and financial assistance. We will also expand our digital learning programs to reach more students.

A challenge we faced

Our model relies on internet access and Zoom, and digital literacy was a real barrier for students in remote districts. We responded by adding Basic IT Learning sessions, so mentees learn to navigate the digital world and use modern educational tools.
